How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings in the app, tap the Pro section, then tap "Restore". Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Why isn't my data syncing?

Ensure iCloud is enabled on all devices and you're signed into the same Apple ID. Try restarting the app or your device.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a request.

Can I export my data?

Yes! Billable Pro includes PDF and CSV export. Go to Settings > Export Data or use the share button in the History tab.
