Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

Introduction

Ember Labs ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Billable mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy applies to all users of the Billable app on iOS and watchOS platforms.

Information We Collect

Data You Provide

  • Project names and client names you create
  • Hourly rates you set for projects
  • Time entries you record (start/end times, duration)
  • App preferences and settings

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App crash reports and performance data (via Apple)
  • Purchase and subscription status (via Apple StoreKit)

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar information
  • Biometric data
  • Advertising identifiers

Data Storage & Security

Local Storage

All your time tracking data (projects, sessions, earnings) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This data is encrypted and protected by your Apple ID credentials. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

No Server Storage

We do not operate servers that store your personal data. Your information stays on your devices and in your personal iCloud account.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information solely to:

  • Provide core app functionality (time tracking, earnings calculation)
  • Sync your data across your devices via iCloud
  • Process in-app purchases through Apple
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Generate aggregated, anonymous analytics

We do NOT use your information to:

  • Display advertisements
  • Create user profiles for marketing
  • Sell or share with third parties
  • Track you across apps or websites

Third-Party Services

Apple Services

We integrate with Apple services for core functionality:

  • App Store / StoreKit: Purchase processing
  • iCloud / CloudKit: Data synchronization
  • App Store Connect: Crash reporting and analytics

Apple's privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy at apple.com/privacy.

No Other Third Parties

We do not integrate with any advertising networks, analytics platforms, or other third-party data collectors.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete it. You can delete individual projects, sessions, or all app data at any time.

Deleting Your Data

  • Delete individual items within the app
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app
  • Remove iCloud data via Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage

We retain no copies of your data on our systems.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored in the app (it's all visible to you)
  • Export your data (via CSV or PDF export features)
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Disable iCloud sync in device settings

Since we don't collect personal data on our servers, there is no data for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
